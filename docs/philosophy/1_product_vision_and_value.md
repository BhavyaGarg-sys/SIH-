# Product Vision & Value Proposition

## The Real Problem
While the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) provides all necessary information online, it is scattered across thousands of dense PDFs, circulars, and different scheme portals. 
For an MSME or a startup trying to launch a product, **the problem isn't just that they can't find the answers—it's that they don't even know what questions to ask.** 
Providing them with a generic "ChatGPT for BIS" (a blank chat box) doesn't solve their pain. They don't know the difference between the ISI Mark Scheme and the Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS). They just want to know: *"I make X, what do I legally need to do to sell it?"*

## The Product Vision
MānaK AI must be a **Guided Compliance Platform**, not just a search bar. The RAG engine is simply the backend brain. The frontend product must feel like a specialized consultant who interviews the user, figures out their needs, and hands them a personalized, actionable compliance roadmap.

## How it Creates Value (The "Aha!" Moment)
Instead of returning a wall of text citing clauses, the platform takes a natural language product description (e.g., *"I'm manufacturing 9W LED bulbs in Gujarat"*) and instantly generates a **Structured Compliance Dashboard**:
1. **The exact standard** they need to follow (e.g., IS 16102).
2. **The exact scheme** they fall under (e.g., CRS).
3. **A step-by-step checklist** (Test at lab -> Register on portal -> Wait for audit).
4. **Actionable next steps** (Here are 3 BIS-approved labs in Gujarat).

## User Personas
1. **The MSME / Startup Founder**: Wants a clear path to certification so they can legally sell their product without hiring expensive consultants.
2. **The Consumer**: Wants to quickly verify if a product they bought is safe/genuine (e.g., checking a hallmark or an ISI mark).
3. **The Student/Researcher**: Wants to deep-dive into the technical specifications of a standard for academic purposes.
